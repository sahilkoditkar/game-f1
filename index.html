About Turbo Tour

Turbo Tour is a free browser racing game: a 3D car game you play instantly, with no download and no sign-up. Drive an open world island in free roam, race on circuits and point-to-point stages, drift through hairpins and earn credits to buy and upgrade your cars.

Two players can race in split screen on the same keyboard (WASD and the arrow keys) or with two gamepads. The game is built with WebGL and Three.js, runs on desktop browsers and works on phones and tablets with on-screen touch controls.

Features

  • Open-world free roam: an island about 6 km across with a city, farmland, forest, desert, an alpine pass and a coast road.
  • 16 circuits with elevation, including layouts inspired by Silverstone, Monza and Spa-Francorchamps.
  • 4 point-to-point stages of 6–10 km: hillclimb, coastal highway, desert and forest rally.
  • 10 cars, from a hot hatch and a rally car to a supercar, a hypercar and an F1 car.
  • 2-player split screen on one keyboard or with two gamepads.
  • Time trial with a ghost car of your best lap.
  • Proper drifting with the handbrake, tyre smoke and skid marks.
  • A career with four championships, prize money, garages and upgrades; progress saves in your browser.

FAQ

Is Turbo Tour free?

Yes. The whole game is free to play, with no ads, no in-app purchases and no account.

Do I need to download anything?

No. It runs directly in your web browser. You need a browser with JavaScript and WebGL, which every current version of Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari has.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes. On phones and tablets touch controls appear automatically. A recent device gives the smoothest frame rate; you can switch graphics to Low in Settings.

Can two people play?

Yes. Split Screen mode lets two players race on one screen: Player 1 uses WASD, Player 2 the arrow keys, or each player uses a gamepad.

Where is my progress saved?

In your browser's local storage on this device. Nothing is uploaded. Clearing site data or using a private window starts a fresh career.
