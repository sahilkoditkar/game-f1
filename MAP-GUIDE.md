# Changing the Free Roam map

The whole island is generated from data in **`src/worlddef.js`**. Edit it, save, and reload the
page: the roads, terrain, buildings, props, full-screen map, radar and GPS all rebuild from it.
Nothing is hand-drawn, so you never edit the map picture itself.

## Coordinates

- Metres. `[x, z]`: **+x is east, +z is south** (the map draws z downward).
- The island is about 6 km across, centred on `[0, 0]`. Apex City is around `[1900, 1700]`.
- To find a spot, drive there in Free Roam, open the browser console (Cmd+Option+J) and run:
  `copy([Math.round(app.horizon.car.pos.x), Math.round(app.horizon.car.pos.z)])`.
  That copies the point to your clipboard.

## Roads: `ROADS`

Each road is one entry:

```js
{ id: 'sunflower', name: 'Sunflower Lane', kind: 'lane', width: 11,
  points: [[180, 1300], [600, 1150], [950, 800], [1250, 700], [1500, 840]] },
```

- `points` are control points. The road is a smooth curve through them, so 4–8 points are
  enough for a long road. More points make tighter bends.
- `kind` sets the look, the traffic speed and the map style: `highway` (yellow, ~18–20 m wide),
  `road` (14 m), `lane` (10–11 m), `dirt` (gravel, 9–10 m), `street` (city, kerbs, 13 m).
- `closed: true` makes a loop (the Horizon Ring and Canyon Loop work this way).
- **Junctions are automatic.** Wherever two roads cross, they get a junction. An open road
  whose end is within **230 m** of another road gets stretched to meet it, so you only need to
  end a road *near* the road it should join.
- To **move** a road, edit its points. To **delete** one, remove its entry. If you delete a road,
  also check the lists further down (`EVENTS`, `TRAPS`, `DRIFTS`, `SPEEDZONES`, `CHAMPIONSHIPS`,
  `GARAGES`) for anything that sits on it (`road: '<id>'`). Otherwise the game fails to load.
- Keep crossings on the same pair of roads at least ~120 m apart. Two closer than that merge
  into one junction.

## Apex City

The city streets are generated by `cityStreets()` in `worlddef.js`:

- `CITY_COLS` gives the x of each north–south street. `CITY_ROWS` gives the z of each
  east–west street. Add, remove or move values to change the grid.
- `NS` / `EW` hold the street names, in the same order as the columns and rows.
- `nsSpan` / `ewSpan` set which rows or columns each street runs between. `[0, 5]` means the
  full length; something like `[1, 5]` stops it short at a T-junction.
- `node()` nudges every crossing by up to 24 m (12 m on the outer streets) so the blocks aren't
  perfect squares. For a dead-straight grid, make it `return [CITY_COLS[i], CITY_ROWS[j]];`.
- The `if (i === …)` / `if (j === …)` lines carry particular streets out of the city to the ring
  road or the coast highway. Edit those points to change the ways in and out.
- Buildings fill whatever space the streets leave, in `_buildCity()` in `src/world.js`. The line
  `const h = dc < 230 ? 45 + hr * 90 : dc < 420 ? 18 + hr * 42 : 9 + hr * 18;` sets the height
  by distance from the city centre: under 230 m tall, under 420 m medium, otherwise low. Change
  those numbers for a taller or flatter skyline.
- Trees and roadside props stay out of the box around the grid (`CITY` plus about 90 m). New
  streets outside that box get countryside props instead.

## Regions, coast, names

- `REGIONS` sets the ground type, terrain shape and big map label of each area: `cx, cz`
  (centre), `r` (radius) and `soft` (blend distance). `lx, lz` place the label.
- `COAST` sets the shape of the coastline. Each entry in `features` is a headland
  (`d` > 0, pushes the land out) or a bay (`d` < 0), at angle `a` (radians, `atan2(z, x)`) with
  width `w`. Its `name` is drawn in the sea on the map.

## Things on the roads

`EVENTS`, `TRAPS`, `DRIFTS`, `SPEEDZONES`, `CHAMPIONSHIPS` and `GARAGES` place markers on a road:

- `road: '<id>'` plus either `at: [x, z]` (snaps to the nearest point on that road; this is
  the sturdier option) or `t: 0.3` (that fraction of the way along the road).
- Drift and speed zones take a start and an end: `at0`/`at1` or `t0`/`t1`.
- Championship venues and garages move along their road to a quiet stretch nearby, so they
  may sit a little away from the `at` you gave.

## Checking your change

1. Reload the page and start Free Roam. If it doesn't load, the browser console shows the error.
   It's usually a `road:` id that no longer exists.
2. Press **M** and zoom in on what you changed.
3. Your saved progress is kept. Fast-travel and waypoint positions are recomputed from the new
   roads.
